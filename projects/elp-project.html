Portfolio / Eastern Legal Partnership

Eastern Legal Partnership

UX/UI Architect & Front-End Consultant

Eastern Legal Partnership was developed as part of Waltham Forest Council's digital commercialisation programme to promote legal services to local authorities and public sector organisations. The website provided a professional digital presence designed to support business development and generate new opportunities for the partnership.

Project Details

Client / Organisation
Eastern Legal Partnership / London Borough of Waltham Forest
Date
2018
Role
UX/UI Architect & Front-End Consultant
Project Focus
Corporate website design, service promotion, lead generation and public sector professional services marketing.
Technical Environment
HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, jQuery, Drupal 8, Responsive Design, UX Design and Agile Delivery.

Project Focus

The project focused on creating a professional corporate website that positioned Eastern Legal Partnership as a trusted provider of legal services to public sector organisations. As part of the wider commercialisation initiative, the site was designed to showcase expertise, service offerings and specialist legal knowledge while encouraging enquiries from prospective clients.

Particular attention was given to presenting complex legal services in a clear and accessible manner. The website needed to balance professionalism and credibility with ease of navigation, ensuring visitors could quickly identify relevant service areas and key contacts.

My Contribution

Working as part of the Waltham Forest Digital Commercial Team, I contributed to the design and front-end delivery of the website, helping to translate business requirements into an effective user experience that reflected the professional nature of the organisation and its services.

Main contributions included:

  • UX/UI architecture and interface design
  • User journey mapping and optimisation
  • Information architecture and content organisation
  • Responsive front-end development
  • Service page and content template design
  • Accessibility and usability improvements
  • Collaboration with stakeholders and content authors
  • Cross-browser and cross-device testing
  • Support for Agile delivery and iterative releases

Technical Environment

The website was delivered using Drupal 8 and a modern front-end technology stack including HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript and jQuery. Responsive design principles were applied throughout to ensure an effective experience across desktop, tablet and mobile devices, while Agile delivery practices enabled close collaboration between stakeholders, designers and developers.

Outcome

The completed website provided Eastern Legal Partnership with a professional digital presence that effectively communicated its legal services and expertise to public sector audiences. Through clear information architecture, responsive design and a strong focus on usability, the platform supported the partnership's objective of promoting its services and generating new business opportunities.
