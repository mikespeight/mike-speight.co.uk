Portfolio / Ford UK

Ford UK

UX/UI Architect & Front-End Consultant

Working as part of a Cognifide delivery team, I supported Ford UK on a programme of digital improvements focused on enhancing the user experience and front-end delivery of customer-facing automotive websites and online services.

Project Details

Client / Organisation
Ford Motor Company UK
Date
2014 – 2015
Role
UX/UI Architect & Front-End Consultant
Project Focus
User experience enhancement, responsive front-end development and digital platform optimisation.
Technical Environment
HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, jQuery, Responsive Design, UX Design, Agile Delivery and Front-End Development.

Project Focus

The engagement focused on improving the user experience and front-end implementation of Ford UK's digital platforms. Working within a large enterprise environment, the objective was to create intuitive customer journeys, improve usability and ensure a consistent experience across desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

The work involved translating business and customer requirements into effective digital solutions while supporting ongoing enhancements to existing platforms. Particular attention was given to responsive design, accessibility, interface consistency and the overall quality of the customer experience.

My Contribution

Following the successful completion of my assignment with The Daily Telegraph, I was retained by Cognifide and assigned to the Ford UK programme. Working alongside designers, developers, stakeholders and delivery teams, I contributed to the UX/UI architecture and front-end implementation of a range of digital initiatives.

Main contributions included:

  • UX/UI architecture and interface design
  • Responsive front-end development
  • User journey mapping and optimisation
  • HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript implementation
  • Cross-browser and cross-device compatibility
  • Accessibility and usability improvements
  • Interface consistency and design refinement
  • Collaboration with business stakeholders and delivery teams
  • Agile delivery support and iterative development
  • Quality assurance and front-end testing

Technical Environment

The project utilised a modern front-end technology stack including HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript and jQuery, together with responsive design techniques and UX best practices. Delivery was undertaken within a collaborative Agile environment involving designers, developers, product stakeholders and business teams.

Project Outcome

The engagement contributed to the ongoing improvement of Ford UK's digital customer experience through enhanced usability, responsive front-end delivery and improved interface consistency. The successful delivery of the assignment further strengthened my relationship with Cognifide and demonstrated my ability to contribute effectively within large-scale enterprise digital programmes.
