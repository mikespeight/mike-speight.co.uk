Portfolio / Mtivity

Mtivity

Senior UX/UI Architect & Digital Delivery Consultant

Mtivity is a Marketing Acceleration Platform designed to help organisations manage marketing workflows, supplier relationships, procurement processes and campaign delivery activities through a single integrated platform.

I worked remotely with Mtivity as part of a small distributed team responsible for enhancing the platform and supporting the delivery of new functionality. The assignment focused on improving the overall user experience of an established product while helping shape the user interface for a number of new modules and features.

The platform contained a wide range of workflow-driven functionality, requiring careful consideration of usability, navigation and information architecture. My role combined UX/UI architecture, front-end implementation and close collaboration with product, technical and business stakeholders to ensure that new features were both practical and easy to use.

Project Focus

The primary focus of the assignment was to enhance the usability and consistency of the existing platform while supporting the design and delivery of new business functionality.

Areas of work included:

  • Guided buying workflows
  • Supplier relationship management
  • Marketing procurement functionality
  • User interface improvements
  • Responsive front-end implementation
  • Platform usability and user experience enhancements

As with many established enterprise platforms, the challenge was not simply to add functionality but to ensure that increasingly complex business processes remained intuitive and efficient for end users.
