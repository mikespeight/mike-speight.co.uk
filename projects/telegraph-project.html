Portfolio / The Daily Telegraph

The Daily Telegraph

UX/UI Architect & Front-End Consultant

Working as part of a Cognifide delivery team, I supported The Daily Telegraph on the enhancement of its digital publishing platforms, helping to improve the user experience, front-end implementation and overall quality of one of the UK's leading news websites.

Project Details

Client / Organisation
The Daily Telegraph
Date
2014
Role
UX/UI Architect & Front-End Consultant
Project Focus
Digital publishing, user experience enhancement, responsive front-end development and online content delivery.
Technical Environment
HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, jQuery, Responsive Design, UX Design, Agile Delivery and Front-End Development.

Project Focus

The engagement focused on enhancing the user experience and front-end implementation of The Daily Telegraph's digital publishing platforms. Operating within a fast-paced media environment, the objective was to improve usability, support content consumption across multiple devices and maintain a high-quality user experience for a large online audience.

Work involved collaborating with editorial, product and technical teams to deliver improvements to existing platforms while supporting the ongoing evolution of the Telegraph's digital presence. Particular emphasis was placed on responsive design, interface consistency and the effective presentation of content.

My Contribution

Working within a multidisciplinary delivery team, I contributed to the design, implementation and refinement of front-end solutions that supported the Telegraph's digital publishing strategy. My role combined UX thinking with practical front-end development, ensuring that user needs and business objectives remained aligned throughout delivery.

Main contributions included:

  • UX/UI architecture and interface refinement
  • Responsive front-end development
  • HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript implementation
  • User journey analysis and optimisation
  • Accessibility and usability improvements
  • Interface consistency and design enhancement
  • Collaboration with editorial, product and technical teams
  • Agile delivery and iterative development
  • Cross-browser and cross-device testing
  • Front-end quality assurance and support

Technical Environment

The project utilised modern front-end technologies including HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript and jQuery, together with responsive design principles and user-centred design practices. Delivery took place within an Agile environment involving close collaboration between editorial, product, design and development teams.

Outcome

The engagement contributed to the continued development and improvement of The Daily Telegraph's digital publishing platforms, helping to enhance the user experience and support content delivery across multiple devices. The successful completion of the assignment led to my subsequent placement by Cognifide on the Ford UK programme, reflecting the confidence placed in my work and delivery capabilities.
