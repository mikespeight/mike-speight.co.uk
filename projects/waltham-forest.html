Waltham Forest Digital Commercialisation Programme

Senior UX/UI Consultant, Scrum Master & Front-End Architect

Following a successful engagement with the London Borough of Croydon, I was invited to join the London Borough of Waltham Forest's Digital Commercialisation Programme. Over a two-year period, our team delivered approximately fifteen websites and digital services designed to promote, support and commercialise a range of council services and initiatives.

Project Details

Client / Organisation
London Borough of Waltham Forest
Date
June 2017 – June 2019
Role
Senior UX/UI Consultant, Scrum Master & Front-End Architect
Project Focus
Digital transformation, service commercialisation, user experience design, Agile delivery and multi-site platform development.
Technical Environment
HTML5, CSS3, Sass, JavaScript, jQuery, Bootstrap, CMS Platforms, Agile Delivery and UX/UI Architecture.

Project Focus

The London Borough of Waltham Forest embarked upon an ambitious programme designed to commercialise selected council services and improve digital engagement with residents, visitors, businesses and partner organisations. The initiative sought to create modern, user-focused websites capable of promoting services, generating revenue opportunities and supporting the council's wider digital transformation objectives.

Working within a small multidisciplinary team, I helped deliver approximately fifteen websites over a two-year period. These ranged from tourism and cultural initiatives through to legal services, environmental attractions and specialist council-led services. Each project required careful consideration of user needs, stakeholder requirements, branding and accessibility while maintaining consistency across the programme.

Several of the most successful projects delivered during the programme included London Borough of Culture, Marry Me London, Eastern Legal Partnership and Walthamstow Wetlands, each supporting a different aspect of the council's commercialisation strategy.

During the programme, approximately fifteen websites and digital services were delivered across a wide range of sectors including culture, tourism, legal services, housing, education and environmental attractions. Technical Environment

The programme utilised a modern front-end technology stack including HTML5, CSS3, Sass, JavaScript, jQuery and responsive design frameworks. Projects were delivered using Agile methodologies with close collaboration between council stakeholders, designers, developers and content teams. The work combined UX architecture, front-end development and Scrum practices to deliver consistent, user-centred digital services across a diverse portfolio of websites.

Project Outcome

The Digital Commercialisation Programme successfully delivered a substantial portfolio of public-facing websites that supported the council's objectives for service promotion, commercial growth and digital transformation. The initiative demonstrated how user-centred design, Agile delivery and modern web technologies could be applied within the public sector to create engaging digital experiences for a wide range of audiences.

The success of the programme led to the continued expansion of digital services across multiple departments and established a strong foundation for future online initiatives within the London Borough of Waltham Forest.
