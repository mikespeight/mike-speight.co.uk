Portfolio / Marry Me London

Marry Me London

UX/UI Architect & Front-End Consultant

Marry Me London was a luxury wedding directory and planning platform designed to connect couples with premium wedding venues and suppliers across London and the South East. I was responsible for designing and delivering an engaging user experience that showcased suppliers while supporting lead generation and enquiries.

Project Details

Client / Organisation
Marry Me London
Date
2017
Role
UX/UI Architect & Front-End Consultant
Project Focus
Website design, user experience architecture, supplier promotion and lead generation.
Technical Environment
HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, jQuery, Responsive Design, UX Design and Front-End Development.

Project Focus

The project focused on creating a visually appealing and highly usable platform that allowed prospective couples to discover wedding venues, photographers, caterers and other wedding suppliers. The user experience needed to balance inspirational content with practical search and navigation functionality while maintaining a premium brand image.

Particular attention was given to content presentation, supplier visibility and the customer journey from initial discovery through to enquiry. The objective was to create a platform that supported both users seeking wedding services and businesses looking to generate qualified leads.

My Contribution

I was responsible for the UX/UI architecture and front-end delivery of the platform, working closely with stakeholders to define the user experience and visual presentation. The role combined user-centred design principles with practical front-end implementation to create a polished and responsive experience across devices.

Main contributions included:

  • UX/UI architecture and interface design
  • User journey planning and optimisation
  • Responsive website design
  • Front-end HTML, CSS and JavaScript development
  • Supplier directory and listing design
  • Search and navigation improvements
  • Content structure and information architecture
  • Cross-device and cross-browser testing

Technical Environment

The platform was delivered using HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript and jQuery, supported by responsive design techniques to ensure an effective user experience across desktop, tablet and mobile devices. Activities covered both design and front-end implementation, with a strong focus on usability, visual presentation and conversion optimisation.

Project Outcome

The completed platform provided an attractive and intuitive environment for couples planning their weddings while helping suppliers increase their visibility and generate enquiries. Through a combination of effective UX design, responsive front-end development and clear content organisation, the project delivered a user experience aligned with the premium nature of the wedding market.
